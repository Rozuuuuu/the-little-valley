# Playtest script: families to kingdoms

**Status:** prepared, **not yet run**. No human has played the kingdom systems yet, and
nothing here has been checked in a browser.

## Setup

- 5–8 people who have not played Little Valley, one at a time, 60–90 minutes each.
- The production build (`npm run build && npm run preview`), mouse and sound, a fresh
  browser profile. Check first at **1366×768** and **1920×1080**.
- Two prepared saves (make them by playing, not by editing files):
  1. **Growth save** — a new valley at about day 2: an orchard planted, a visitor
     waiting, five adults.
  2. **Realm save** — a Region-level valley: two settlements joined by a route, an inn,
     a crowned ruler with an Envoy and a Marshal, two known kingdoms (one friendly), a
     barracks, swords and a trained company, Civilization reached.
- Screen and voice recording (with consent) and the observer sheet below.

## What you say

> "This is a settlement game that grows into a small kingdom. Please think aloud: what
> you are looking at, what you are trying to do, what surprises you. I can't help during
> play, but I'll answer everything afterwards."

Don't coach. If someone is stuck for 3 minutes, note it and ask only "What would you
try next?"

## Tasks (never read out; watch whether they happen)

From the growth save:

| # | Watch for | Success looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Recruiting | Plants/uses the orchard and welcomes a visitor; can say what apples were for |
| 2 | Children | Starts a household, asks for a child, explains why the child doesn't work |

From the realm save:

| # | Watch for | Success looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | A stalled mine or route | Finds why a mine or caravan route stopped (storage, teamster, bridge) and fixes it |
| 4 | Protected land | Can point to the protected homeland and say what it protects against |
| 5 | Rumour vs fact | Tells a rumour from a confirmed report in the News tab and says who told them |
| 6 | A worried neighbour | Answers a warning (reassure, investigate or negotiate) and explains the choice |
| 7 | Allied support | Asks an ally for soldiers, compares their counter-offer, and explains promised vs arrived strength |
| 8 | Retreat or peace | Pulls a company home or makes peace, and says what happens to held land and captives |

Also watch for **accidental commitments**: clicking Declare war, ending a treaty early,
or accepting a counter-offer without meaning to.

## Observer sheet

| Time | What happened | Quote | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | confusion / failed click / accidental commitment / waiting / delight / wish |

Record especially: repeated failed clicks (and where), confusion about which panel to
open, waiting with nothing to do, buildings they wished for, and why they would or
wouldn't come back tomorrow.

## Feedback form (1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree)

1. **Clarity:** I understood what I could do next.
2. **Controls:** Selecting, ordering and the panels were easy to use.
3. **Art:** The valley and its new buildings looked charming and readable.
4. **Pacing:** Growth (visitors, children) felt worth the wait, not slow.
5. **Diplomacy:** I understood why other kingdoms acted as they did.
6. **Continuing:** I want to come back to this same kingdom tomorrow.
7. What was the most confusing moment?
8. What did you expect to be able to do but couldn't?

## Accessibility checks (by the facilitator, before sessions)

- Every panel reachable and usable with the keyboard; focus visible.
- Text contrast readable on the dark panels; warnings not signalled by colour alone
  (they also carry ⚠ and words).
- Selection, placement previews, borders and occupied land visible at every zoom.
- Every panel has a close or back control; pause works with panels open; sounds can
  be muted.

## Afterwards

Group notes by task. Anything that stopped two or more players is a must-fix. Report
only sessions that actually happened.
